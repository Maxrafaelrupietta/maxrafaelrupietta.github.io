Privacy

Last updated: October 8, 2026. This policy covers the iPhone app “Watt Tycoon” and this website. The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

Max Rupietta, Kusenhorster Straße 24A, 45721 Haltern am See, Germany, email: maxrafaelrupietta@gmail.com (see imprint).

2. In short

Your game progress stays on your device. There is no account and no server of our own. For ads, statistics and crash reports the app uses third-party services. Data for personalized ads and statistics is only processed with your consent, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's settings.

3. Game progress and settings

Progress, gems, purchases and settings are stored locally on your device. They don't leave it, except in a backup you set up yourself in iOS.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We receive no payment data, only Apple's confirmation of which product was bought.

5. Game Center and notifications

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, leaderboard scores and achievements are sent to Apple. Notifications are local: they are scheduled on your device and only shown if you allowed them.

6. Consent

Once ads unlock in the game, we ask for your consent: in the EU/EEA through an IAB TCF consent message (Google User Messaging Platform), then through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can withdraw consent at any time in the game under Settings → “Privacy Settings”, and for tracking in the iOS settings.

7. Ads: Google AdMob

The app only shows voluntary rewarded videos through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland; parent: Google LLC, USA). This processes device information (model, OS, language), IP address, approximate location and ad interactions, and with your consent the advertising ID (IDFA). Through AdMob mediation, other ad networks may bid for the ad. Without consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown. More: How Google uses data for ads.

8. Statistics, crash reports, remote config: Google Firebase

  • Google Analytics for Firebase – how the game is played (e.g. goals reached, play time, tutorial steps) to improve it. Only with consent; without it, no identifiers are stored.
  • Crashlytics – crash reports (device model, OS, code location, a random installation ID) so we can fix bugs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working app.
  • Remote Config – loads game values (e.g. ad rewards) without an app update, using a random installation ID. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland (parent: Google LLC, USA). More: Firebase privacy.

9. Install attribution: AppsFlyer

With your consent we use AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd., Israel, and AppsFlyer Inc., USA) to measure which ad campaign led to the install and to attribute purchases and a few game events (e.g. “tutorial completed”) to campaigns. More: AppsFlyer privacy policy.

10. International transfers

Some providers process data in the USA or Israel. Israel has an EU adequacy decision. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, otherwise on EU standard contractual clauses.

11. Retention

Local game data stays until you delete the app. Providers keep data according to their own periods; Google Analytics at most 14 months.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

13. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

14. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which processes technically necessary data such as IP addresses in server logs. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no fonts from third-party servers.
